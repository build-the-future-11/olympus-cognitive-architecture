# Olympus v6: conservative disjoint stacking

This successor is authorized by the user's 2026-10-21 full-pipeline request.
The machine-readable `protocol.json` is authoritative. Versions 6.0.0 and
6.0.1 are preserved pre-execution drafts; 6.0.2 incorporates independent review.
No successor data or scientific result was observed while making those changes.

## Question and contribution

Does an example-dependent, penalized intervention around a frozen strong
reference improve accuracy on a reserved synthetic shift configuration without
materially harming clean or familiar-corruption accuracy? The contribution is a
controlled empirical decomposition of anchoring and worst-group training. The
convex mixture itself is established mixture-of-experts/stacking methodology,
not a new general architecture or a risk guarantee.

The predecessor record supports corruption coverage and one residual temporal
predictor, not general routing superiority. All prior routing failures remain
terminal. This study does not train target-scale six-family models, change
their promotion status, or establish natural-data performance.

## Exact model and chronological fitting

1. Generate 1,024 new broad-corruption examples per independent dataset seed.
   Two Olympus v4 ExpertBank instances each fit one parity half, 250 updates,
   batch 64, AdamW lr .001, decay .01, gradient clip 1.0. The loss is the mean
   five-expert class cross-entropy plus .25 times temporal MSE; CF weight is zero.
2. Freeze both banks. For every subsequent example, average their five expert
   class-probability vectors and average their five 16-dimensional states.
   The router sees 80 state coordinates, 20 probabilities and five entropies.
   This identical ensemble construction is used at fitting and inference.
3. Fit reference semantic routing (LayerNorm105, Linear105x32, GELU, Linear32x5,
   softmax) and five fixed mixing logits for 300 updates on `reference_fit`.
   Reference options also include uniform and each of five single experts.
   Choose reference on separate `reference_select`, using worst-regime accuracy,
   then mean accuracy, then declared order. Freeze this choice before correction.
4. Each alternative copies the semantic reference weights, explicitly re-enables
   their gradients, and receives 300 updates on separate `correction_fit` data.
   Direct variants use the alternative. Constant variants learn one scalar
   intervention logit. Adaptive variants use a 105-to-1 linear logit; weights
   start at zero and bias is logit(.1). Final probability is
   `(1-alpha)*reference + alpha*alternative`.
5. Mean-loss training equally averages three group mean NLLs. Robust training
   minimizes their maximum with deterministic torch.max subgradient. This is
   minibatch worst-group loss, not adaptive-weight group DRO. Penalized constant
   and adaptive variants add `.01*mean(alpha)`; no-penalty adaptive robust is a
   separate ablation. Every update draws 32 examples per group with identical
   sampling across variants. All use the same optimizer, decay and clipping.
6. Freeze all checkpoints, then use a separate `selection` partition to choose
   the strongest declared control by the same rule. The primary candidate is
   fixed in advance as `anchored_robust`; no best-candidate test selection.

The bank/reference/correction/reference-selection/final-selection partitions are
source-disjoint. This is disjoint stacked fitting, not nested cross-fitting.
Source identities are seed*100000 + example index. Dataset seeds are spaced by
100 and the declared stage offsets are disjoint. Two training seeds share data
within each independent dataset cluster. The exact seed lists are in JSON.

## Data, rights and shifts

All tensors are generated by repository-owned deterministic source; no external
data, base weights or licenses are silently substituted. Development groups are
clean, familiar corrupted and known shift. Protected data use fresh offsets
and are generated only after all ten development cells and the scientific
freeze exist. Every arm uses identical protected tensors.

`composite_shift` reserves a two-token relocation, alternating-row image
occlusion at .5, or state scaling .5 plus a time-linear bias from -.75 to .75,
applied to the damaged modality while preserving source identity and targets.
These are reserved configurations of historically observed transformation
types. The generator damages one modality per example; the name does not mean
all perturbations occur together. There is no novel-family or natural-OOD claim.

## Controls, metrics, statistics and decision

Controls include uniform, fixed, semantic, each individual expert, direct ERM,
direct robust, constant ERM and constant robust. Additional ablations are
adaptive ERM and adaptive robust without the intervention penalty. Direct
controls receive the same extra 300 updates, avoiding an extra-training claim.
Adaptive alpha adds 106 parameters, constant alpha adds one, direct adds none;
report these differences and do not claim a perfectly parameter-matched result.

Primary: adaptive robust minus development-selected control on composite-shift
accuracy, mean >= .02 and paired descriptive 95% interval lower bound > 0.
Clean, corrupted and known-shift nonregression must each hold against BOTH the
selected control and frozen reference: mean >= -.01 and interval lower >= -.02.
All gates are conjunctive. Failure is a negative primary result, regardless of
secondary winners. Selection controls are not an oracle best protected control.

Average two optimizer seeds within each of five independent dataset clusters.
Bootstrap the five paired cluster deltas 10,000 times with the fixed analysis
seed; report every cluster. These intervals are descriptive pilot uncertainty,
not precise inferential or safety guarantees. Other comparisons are secondary.

Record accuracy, NLL, Brier, ECE, selective accuracy, intervention amount,
rescues, harms and prediction changes. `(rescues-harms)/N` must equal the
accuracy difference against the reference. Retain individual expert outputs,
all probability/alpha tensors, source IDs, labels, hashes and failure logs.

## Validity, budgets and stopping

One scientific campaign: 5 datasets x 2 optimizer seeds = 10 cells, 20 bank
fits, 40 protected regime reports. No scientific development revisions or
hyperparameter search are admitted in this version. Artificial qualification
is separate and must pass on the exact source before execution. Hard outer
timeouts: 120 seconds qualification, 3,600 seconds campaign. CPU, one Torch
thread, deterministic algorithms; zero paid jobs and zero external downloads.
Resource admission requires 2 GiB available and <=90% swap; macOS additionally
requires >=512 MiB free and stable pageouts, checked during work. Preserve
incomplete/invalid runs. No threshold relaxation or favorable rerun is allowed.

Before protected generation, bind protocol, exact source, environment, data
identities, reference/control selections and every checkpoint/report. Verification
regenerates data, reloads all banks and routers, replays every prediction and
intervention exactly, and recomputes metrics and summary. This is computational
replay, not independent retraining. A source change invalidates the freeze.

```sh
python -m scripts.run_conservative_routing_v6 verify --output artifacts/v6-campaign-NEW
```

Use immutable new directories. Do not run `--worker` directly: that is the
internal subprocess governed by the public command's hard deadline.
