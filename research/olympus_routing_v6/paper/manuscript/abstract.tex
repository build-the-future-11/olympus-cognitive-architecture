\begin{abstract}
We specify a bounded successor to a sequence of unsuccessful Olympus routing
experiments. The model mixes a frozen, development-selected reference with a
learned alternative, using an example-dependent intervention penalty and
worst-group training. Seven correction variants and strong single-expert,
fixed, uniform, direct, and constant-blend controls isolate competing
explanations. Five independently generated datasets, each with two optimizer
seeds, support paired descriptive analysis under four synthetic regimes.
The primary hypothesis requires at least two percentage points of accuracy
gain on a reserved shift configuration and explicit nonregression elsewhere.
Methods were written before scientific execution. At this version, the new
scientific study is not evaluated; artificial qualification is engineering
evidence only. Earlier negative routing endpoints remain preserved.
\end{abstract}
