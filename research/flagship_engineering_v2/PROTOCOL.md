# Six flagship engineering successor v2

Frozen engineering scope: 2026-10-19, before implementation or new evaluation.

The user requests six flagship improvements, JEPA parameter growth, bounded lazy
ghost residency, and preparation for Kaggle training and benchmarking. Prometheus
is a 12–20B target and Kronos is an 8T target. Targets are distinct from allocated,
resident, active, and trained parameters. Atlas and Aion scales remain unspecified.

## Changes and falsifiers

1. Hermes: two-view tool agreement may raise abstention; a host tool mask must
   forbid selection. Falsified by unauthorized probability mass or reduced abstention.
2. Prometheus: prefix-causal adapter routing with disagreement-limited residuals.
   Falsified when changing future tokens changes earlier logits or the cap is exceeded.
3. Perseus: routing constrained to an explicit resident expert set. Falsified by
   executing an unavailable expert, processing padding, or accepting an insufficient set.
4. Atlas: explicit patch-evidence exclusion. Falsified by excluded-patch influence
   or probability mass, or by silently accepting an empty evidence set.
5. Kronos: evolving residual state rollouts with cumulative nonnegative uncertainty.
   Falsified by frozen rollout state, invalid padded outputs, or decreasing uncertainty.
6. Aion: intersect host authority with per-transition resource affordability.
   Falsified by an unaffordable transition or a STOP fallback that lacks host authority.
7. Olympus: combine per-family evidence diagnostics into a monotone risk envelope,
   recompute Aion once for the final decision, and expose the contribution of each term.
8. JEPA parameter growth: function-preserving feedforward widening plus a masked
   latent-prediction adapter with a frozen target encoder. Growth is initialization,
   not learned knowledge or evidence that a target-scale model was trained.
9. Ghost residency: reject forged plans and unsafe paths, account for tensor storage,
   and offer inference leases that return modules to meta after use, including errors.
10. Kaggle: prepare an offline, source-bound artificial qualification notebook,
    explicit CPU/CUDA selection, finite resource/time budgets, and immutable receipts.

## Acceptance

Adversarial and numerical regression tests must cover each falsifier, checkpoint
round trips, finite training gradients, growth parity, and lease cleanup. Retain
failed attempts. Run Ruff, strict MyPy, the existing full suite, readiness baseline,
and package checks under the unchanged host governor. Preserve all prior scientific
outcomes and source identities. Archive the preceding engineering receipt before
rolling its current pointer forward.

## Budgets and evidence

Local artificial examples: at most four per numerical fixture; tensor dimensions
at most 64 except vocabulary and test-only serialization fixtures. Per qualification
job: at most eight optimizer steps, 120 seconds, one CPU thread; optional one CUDA
device. No paid compute, external dataset download, protected evaluation, or family
promotion in this engineering phase. CPU and CUDA results are distinct.

The prepared Kaggle job qualifies these development-scale mechanisms only. A later
LLM campaign requires selected revision-pinned base weights, licensed data, immutable
splits, fixed controls, metrics, stopping rules, and a compute admission calculation.
An 8T parameter declaration does not authorize or make an 8T allocation feasible.

## Novelty boundary

These are candidate Olympus-specific feature combinations. JEPA (Assran et al.,
2023), function-preserving widening (Chen et al., 2015), sparse routing, abstention,
and parameter offloading are existing ideas. No priority or scientific novelty
claim is established by implementation. Each successor needs an ablation and a
literature comparison before a scientific contribution can be claimed.
