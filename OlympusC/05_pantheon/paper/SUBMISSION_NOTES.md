# Submission Positioning Notes

## Current defensible positioning

Pantheon is strongest as a **reproducibility / evaluation methodology** paper, not as a claim of solved autonomous science. The 2026 NeurIPS ecosystem explicitly recognizes reproducibility research, including AI-assisted reproducibility and reproducibility of AI systems/agents, making the topic well aligned in spirit.

## What a main-track-quality next revision still needs

- real independent LLM research agents from at least two model/provider families;
- at least one third-party frozen research package or tasks drawn from CORE-Bench, PaperBench, or AutoExperiment;
- natural failure localization, not only injected faults;
- human adjudicator baseline;
- blinded vs. unblinded replication comparison;
- cross-model/shared-context false-consensus experiment;
- stronger isolation via containers or VMs;
- preregistered decision thresholds before the agent benchmark run.

The repository should not be submitted with language implying those experiments have already occurred.
