# Research Truth

## Current scientific status

Pantheon is an executable auditing prototype with two kinds of evidence:

1. controlled mechanism tests on synthetic faults and a public-data cross-implementation case study; and
2. a completed, frozen CORE-Bench v1.1 OOD run using two small local language models.

The OOD artifact is complete, but its scientific result is negative. Agent A (`qwen3:0.6b`) and Agent B (`llama3.2:1b`) each answered **0/20** canonical questions and each produced **0/17** agent-authored valid reports (**0/34** trajectories combined). The post-study capability floor therefore failed. The study does not estimate false-consensus prevalence among capable research agents.

The authoritative interpretation gate is [`poststudy/SCIENTIFIC_READINESS.md`](poststudy/SCIENTIFIC_READINESS.md), whose verdict is `NEGATIVE_RESULT_ARTIFACT_COMPLETE_CAPABILITY_FLOOR_FAILED`. The older [`PUBLICATION_READINESS.md`](PUBLICATION_READINESS.md) is a frozen structural artifact-completeness record and is superseded for scientific or venue-readiness claims.

## What was actually run

- One clean smoke reproduction package: proposer and replicator executed in separate fresh temporary workspaces and matched exactly.
- Controlled seeded-fault benchmark: **200 subprocess executions** across 20 synthetic seeds and 8 comparison conditions, totaling 160 proposer/replicator comparisons and 480 baseline/adjudicator evaluations.
- Public-data cross-implementation case study: **20 model executions** (2 implementations × 10 prespecified stratified splits) on the Breast Cancer Wisconsin Diagnostic dataset bundled with scikit-learn.
- Frozen CORE-Bench v1.1 OOD study: **17 tasks, 20 questions, and 34 isolated local-agent trajectories** across four fields and Python/R capsules.
- Evidence validation and post-study readiness checks that preserve failed, null, and harness-normalized outputs instead of treating them as successful agent submissions.

## What succeeded

- Full Pantheon fault-localization accuracy on the controlled benchmark: **1.000 (160/160)**.
- Pairwise-artifact baseline: **0.625 (100/160)**.
- Numeric-only baseline: **0.125 (20/160)**.
- Shared-bug case: both weaker baselines falsely label all 20 shared-bug cases as reproduced; canonical Pantheon detects implementation drift in all 20.
- Public case direction agreement: **1.000 (10/10)** for improvement over majority accuracy.
- Public case mean absolute proposer/replicator effect difference: **0.006993** accuracy points.
- The frozen OOD harness completed **17/17 tasks and 34/34 trajectories** with distinct model identities, separate ephemeral workspaces, canonical-answer withholding, normalized failure artifacts, and no recorded provider errors.

These successes verify mechanism behavior and artifact capture. They do not establish autonomous scientific replication quality.

## What failed or was constrained

- Both local agents produced **0/20 non-null answers** and **0/20 correct answers**.
- Neither agent authored a valid report on any task: Agent A **0/17**, Agent B **0/17**. The 34 `report.json` files used for artifact completeness were generated by harness failure normalization, not submitted by the agents.
- With no substantive answers, there were **0/20 substantive agreements** and **0/20 observed false-consensus events**. That zero is not evidence of a low false-consensus rate among capable agents; the target conditional prevalence is unidentified in this run.
- The Wilson 95% upper bound for each observed zero question-level event rate is approximately **0.161**, but this does not repair the failed capability floor or justify population-wide claims.
- The two agents used different local model identities but the same Ollama provider and host. Provider diversity, separately administered infrastructure, and frontier-agent capability were not tested.
- The 17-task CORE-Bench subset was selected to reach 20 questions while excluding two very large capsules. It is neither random nor representative of the full benchmark.
- No Research Atlas or other third-party frozen research package supplied independently of the benchmark was present, so independent reproduction of a published claim outside CORE-Bench remains incomplete.

## Strongest supported claims

> On the controlled seeded-fault suite, canonical frozen-package auditing with provenance and canary checks localizes the injected failure class more accurately than numeric-only or proposer-vs-replicator pairwise comparison, and uniquely detects correlated shared implementation drift.

> On the frozen 17-task CORE-Bench v1.1 OOD subset, the local harness completed all planned trajectories, but both evaluated small models failed to answer any of the 20 questions or author any of the 34 required reports. This is a bounded negative capability result, not evidence about false consensus among capable agents.

## Claims that cannot currently be made

- Pantheon makes autonomous AI science reliable.
- Pantheon reduces false consensus among capable research agents.
- Pantheon has been validated across providers, independently administered hosts, or frontier model families.
- Pantheon is state of the art on CORE-Bench, PaperBench, AutoExperiment, or any external agent benchmark.
- Pantheon has independently reproduced a published research claim end to end.
- The controlled 100% localization result estimates real-world failure-localization accuracy.
- The observed 0/20 false-consensus count establishes a zero or low false-consensus prevalence.
- The repository is publication-ready for a positive cross-agent scientific claim.

## Readiness-gate hierarchy

The frozen V4 structural gate wrote `PUBLICATION-READY` after confirming task/question counts, identity separation, OOD labeling, artifact presence, and absence of provider errors. Its `complete_reports` check tested whether normalized report files existed; it did not test whether agents authored valid reports or answered the benchmark questions.

That label is retained as historical evidence of what the frozen gate emitted, but it is not the current scientific verdict. The post-study gate explicitly checks minimum answered-question and agent-authored-report floors and supersedes it for interpretation. Its failure must remain visible in any report, abstract, or readiness claim.
